Our Team

NANOCARD is an interdisciplinary team working at the interface of nanomaterials, redox biology and cardiovascular medicine, bringing together postdoctoral researchers, doctoral students and international collaborators across nanomaterials synthesis, vascular biology and translational biomedical engineering.

Dr Tanveer Tabish

Principal Investigator · Senior Research Fellow & Group Leader

Cardiovascular nanoscientist working at the interface of nanomaterials, redox chemical biology and biomedical engineering. He leads NANOCARD in the Division of Cardiovascular Medicine, Radcliffe Department of Medicine, University of Oxford, and is a Fellow of Kellogg College. His group develops nitric oxide-releasing nanomedicines for cardiovascular repair, redox- and pH-responsive drug carriers, platforms for real-time imaging of nitric oxide in cells, and next-generation cardiovascular implants with controlled, stimulus-responsive release.

Join the Group

We welcome enquiries from talented researchers who wish to work at the interface of nanomaterials, redox biology and cardiovascular therapeutics. Prospective DPhil students interested in cardiovascular nanomedicine, biomaterials or redox biology are encouraged to get in touch, as are postdoctoral researchers applying for independent fellowships or looking to join ongoing projects.
