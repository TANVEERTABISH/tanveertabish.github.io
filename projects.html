Nitric Oxide-Releasing Nanomedicines

University of Oxford · NANOCARD

Nitric oxide sits at the heart of vascular homeostasis, yet its therapeutic potential remains constrained by challenges in spatiotemporal delivery and targeted bioactivity. We engineer nanomaterials that release NO where and when it is needed, restoring endothelial function and promoting vascular healing.

Redox-Responsive Drug Carriers

University of Oxford · NANOCARD

We develop redox-responsive nanodrug excipients capable of navigating the biochemical gradients of the cardiovascular system, using pH-selective permeabilities to guide cargo to subcellular targets in cardiovascular disease.

Real-Time Imaging of Nitric Oxide

University of Oxford · NANOCARD

Advanced synthetic platforms for imaging NO at subcellular resolution, illuminating how NO fluxes relate to mitochondrial dysfunction, oxidative stress and endothelial pathology in living cells.
