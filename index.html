Key Research Areas

We advance nanomaterial-enabled cardiovascular medicine as a platform for solving unmet clinical challenges; coupling materials design, redox chemical biology and device engineering to accelerate solutions in vascular healing, targeted delivery and real-time diagnosis. Our group cultivates an environment where rigorous science and bold ideas converge.

Dr Tanveer Tabish

Principal Investigator · Senior Research Fellow & Group Leader, University of Oxford

A cardiovascular nanoscientist working at the interface of nanomaterials, redox chemical biology and biomedical engineering. His work focuses on nitric oxide-releasing nanomedicines for cardiovascular repair, redox-responsive drug carriers, real-time imaging of NO in cells, and next-generation cardiovascular implants, in the Radcliffe Department of Medicine at the University of Oxford.
