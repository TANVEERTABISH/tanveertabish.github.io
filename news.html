DPhil Projects

🧬 Three Fully Funded DPhil Projects Available

We currently have three DPhil projects on offer through two different Oxford programmes, for students interested in cardiovascular nanomedicine, nitric oxide biology and functional biomaterials. Details of each programme are below.

⏳ Applications for both programmes close on 1 December 2026.
2026Applications Open
BHF Oxford CRE

Photoresponsive Nanotherapeutics for Vascular Healing

A fully funded DPhil studentship through the BHF Oxford Centre of Research Excellence (CRE) graduate studentship scheme, supervised jointly with Professor Matthew Fuchter and Professor Keith Channon. The project develops light-activated nanotherapeutics for controlled, site-specific vascular healing.

⏳ Application deadline: 1 December 2026

BHF VascularRewind

BHF Oxford–Cambridge VascularRewind Programme

Fully funded DPhil projects through the BHF Oxford–Cambridge VascularRewind Programme, a four-year structured research degree on the secondary prevention of vascular dysfunction and disease. Our projects focus on nitric oxide-releasing nanomedicines for restoring endothelial function and vascular homeostasis.

⏳ Application deadline: 1 December 2026

The Kroto Medal 2025
